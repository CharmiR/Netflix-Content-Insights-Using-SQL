# Netflix-Content-Insights-Using-SQL
This project applies advanced SQL queries to analyze Netflix’s global content catalog with the goal of uncovering insights that can drive business growth. It explores viewing trends, genre popularity, regional production strengths, and actor/director impact to help media platforms and content teams make informed, data-driven decisions.
# Key Business Insights:
1)Content Strategy: 
Understand viewer preferences (e.g., movies vs TV shows, top genres, most-watched countries).

2)Talent Impact: 
Identify high-performing actors and directors to guide casting and partnerships.

3)Regional Trends: 
Analyze country-specific content growth to support localization and expansion.

4)Catalog Optimization: 
Spot outdated or underperforming content and recommend fresh additions.

5)User Safety: 
Flag violent or sensitive content for better parental controls and personalized filtering.
